Lens types and their abbreviations:

Convex lens
Abbreviation: CVX
Description: A convex lens directs light towards a central focal point. It's usually used for long-throw lights.

Concave lens
Abbreviation: CCV
Description: A concave lens spreads light out. It's often used in optical systems to make images smaller.

Polycarbonate lens
Abbreviation: PC
Description: Very impact-resistant and light. Often used as the lens in torches, protective gear and cameras.

Aspheric lens
Abbreviation: ASP
Description: A lens with a non-spherical surface that reduces distortion and improves image quality. Often used in optical and camera systems.

TIR (Total Internal Reflection) lens
Abbreviation: TIR
Description: A TIR lens guides light using total internal reflection, for efficient beam control.

Biconvex lens
Abbreviation: BCVX
Description: Convex on both sides, which gives a stronger focusing effect.

Tempered glass lens
Abbreviation: TGL
Description: A tempered glass lens is especially impact-resistant and stands up very well to mechanical damage. Mainly used in torches, cameras and other technical devices.

Fresnel, like on overhead projectors

A diffusion layer is usually abbreviated DF (Diffusion Filter) or DL (Diffusion Layer), but other labels are used too, depending on the industry.
A diffusion layer is a special coating or surface structure on a lens that spreads the light more evenly, reducing glare and hot spots.

Reflector types and their abbreviations:

Parabolic reflector
Abbreviation: PR
Description: A parabolic reflector is bowl-shaped and gathers the light into a single central focal point.

Smooth reflector
Abbreviation: SMO
Description: A smooth reflector has a smooth, shiny surface that concentrates the light and gives a long throw.

Orange peel reflector
Abbreviation: OP
Description: A textured surface that looks like orange peel. It spreads the light gently, so the beam is more even.

Hybrid reflector
Abbreviation: HYB
Description: A hybrid reflector combines the advantages of smooth and textured surfaces, giving both long throw and a beam without dark rings.

Elliptical reflector
Abbreviation: ER
Description: An elliptical reflector has two focal points and sends the light in different directions.

Polished reflector
Abbreviation: PLR
Description: A polished reflector has a very smooth, shiny surface for maximum reflection.

TIR (Total Internal Reflection) optics
Abbreviation: TIR
Description: TIR optics guide the light by internal reflection, through a closed optical system.
