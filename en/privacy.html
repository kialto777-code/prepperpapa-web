Privacy notice

prepperpapa.hu | Effective: 28 October 2026

In short

By default this site places no cookies on your device. We ask for your consent for two purposes, which you can grant or refuse separately: (1) in our partner programme, to determine which partner is owed a commission if you buy; (2) for anonymous visitor statistics (Google Analytics). If you decline, everything still works. There is no registration and no newsletter. We ask for personal data only if you sign up yourself for the notice about the app's launch, or buy.

There are four things you should know about: the server's technical log, embedded videos, partner links, and the launch notification. The few paragraphs below describe them.

Contact

1. Server log

The website runs on the GitHub Pages service. When you open a page, the server automatically logs your IP address for security purposes. We have no access to this log and do not link it to anything.

  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the website available and secure (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Who processes it: GitHub B.V. (Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam) and GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, USA).

The data may also be processed in the United States; for this they rely on the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

  • How long: decided by the provider, which does not publish a fixed retention period.

2. Embedded videos and content

Some articles contain embedded YouTube videos. Until you start one, no cookie is placed on your device.

Once you start it, Google's own privacy policy applies to you from then on.

Two articles also contain other embedded content (a Google document and a manufacturer's image). When these load, your browser connects to the provider concerned, and your IP address reaches it in the process.

3. Cookies and consent

On your first visit a window offers two categories, each with equally prominent "Accept" and "Reject" buttons. Until you decide, no cookie is placed on your device. You can change your choice at any time under "Cookie settings" in the footer, or by deleting cookies in your browser. We remember your choice in your browser's local storage (key pp-suti, this site only, 365 days; if the set of cookies changes we ask again). The legal basis for both categories is your consent (section 155(4) of Act C of 2003 on Electronic Communications; if the data counts as personal data, GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

3.1. Partner (affiliate) tracking

Our partners share links that contain their code (?ref=...). If you arrive from such a link and consent, we place the following cookies on the prepperpapa.hu domain:

  • our own cookie (pp_ref): the partner's code and the time of the click;
  • the tracking cookies of the GoAffPro partner-programme service (set by the GoAffPro loader): ref (the partner's code), gfp_ref_expires, gfp_landing_page (the first page you landed on), gfp_referrer (where you came from), gfp_session_start (start of the visit); ref and gfp_ref_expires also appear in the browser's local storage; in some flows a dcode cookie for a coupon code alongside.

Purpose: solely to determine which partner is owed a commission if you buy within 90 days. Duration: 90 days from the last partner click; another partner's link replaces the old one. Our own cookie holds nothing that refers to your name, e-mail address or payment. If you do not consent: the site and purchases work the same, but no link-based partner commission arises; if you use a partner's coupon code, the partner is credited anyway, because that does not rely on a cookie.

3.2. Visitor statistics (Google Analytics 4)

If you consent, we use Google Analytics 4 (provider: Google Ireland Limited) to see anonymously how many visitors come, which pages they view and where they come from. Cookies: _ga (distinguishes visitors) and _ga_K4K2L8JMN5 (session state). According to Google's documentation their default lifetime is 2 years; we set it to at most 14 months (browsers cap it at 400 days, so that is the effective lifetime), and the retention of event data and user-level data is also 14 months; it restarts with each new visit, counted from the last visit. According to Google, GA4 does not log your IP address; we state this on the basis of Google's description. Data may be transferred to the United States; the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified. We do not use it for advertising or profiling and do not combine it with other data. If you do not consent, GA4 is not loaded at all.

4. Notification when the app launches

On the PrepperPapa app page you can enter your e-mail address so we can let you know when the PrepperPapa app is available to buy. We use the address solely for this one notification, and keep it until we have sent the launch notification or until you ask us to delete it at prepperpapa@gmail.com.

  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

5. What we do not do

No advertising cookies, no profiling and no automated decision-making. We use cookies only as described in section 3, after your consent, which is why the site shows a cookie consent banner. We do not collect names or phone numbers for the sign-up, we use e-mail addresses only for the launch notice, and we do not pass data on for marketing.

You can request access to the data concerning you, its rectification, erasure or restriction, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and exercise your right to data portability. Write to the address above.

Apart from the e-mail address for the launch notification, we do not store any data that could identify you, so we can only act on other requests if you yourself give us something to go on. The server log is subject to the provider's own procedures.
