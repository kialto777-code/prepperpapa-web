Wordlight · PrepperPapa Bible

The Bible in your pocket, even without internet.

Wordlight is the PrepperPapa Bible app. You can read it and listen to it, and after a single download it works without internet too. It installs separately, next to PrepperPapa.

01

The Bible in your language

In every language the familiar, well-known translation, free to download. Switch between the downloaded translations with one tap.

02

Listen

  • Verse numbers are not read out; the pace and the pause between verses can be set, and background music can play underneath.
  • Read aloud with the phone's voice or the Studio voice (a recorded human narration). The Studio voice of every book can be downloaded free, for offline listening too. Free users get 5 minutes of read-aloud a day, unlimited with Bible Premium. Prayers and the morning alarm read-aloud do not count.
  • Prayers and confessions can be read aloud too.
03

Read, mark, follow

  • Search by word or by reference, accents optional.
  • Colour highlights, your own commentary, bookmarks and sharing for every verse.
  • Cross-references and Bible maps: places with descriptions, Paul's journeys and your own position.
  • Eight reading plans, several at once, with progress tracking: the whole Bible in a year or in 90 days, the New Testament, the Gospels, Psalms and Proverbs.
  • Auto-scroll, text size, book colours and a red night mode.
04

Wake up with Scripture

The alarm can be tied to a reading plan, on the days of the week you choose. On Android it wakes the phone and reads the day's passage aloud.

05

Games

  • Verse games in adult and child mode, with points and streaks: Verse quiz, Who said it?, Where is it?, Reference match and Book order.
  • In child mode, four picture games for little ones: Memory game, Noah's ark, Who is who? and Puzzle.
06

Prayer and emergencies

  • Built-in and your own prayers, a prayer timer with a soft bell; the screen stays on while you pray.
  • Verses for emergencies for thirty life situations, and prophecies grouped by theme.

What is free, what is Bible Premium?

Bible Premium gives unlimited read-aloud time and the original word with one tap. Everything else is free, including downloading the recorded voices and all the background music. Price: €24.99, one-time purchase, permanent. Christmas price until 6 January 2027: €19.99 (7,777 Ft when paying in forints).
