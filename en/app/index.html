Tour

Ten screens that matter.

01 · Readiness

One glance, and you know where you stand.

Water, food, heating, power: by location, as a percentage. If something is running low, you also see what to restock.

02 · Map

You know the way, even in the woods.

Hiking trails, springs, mountain huts. Route plans with distance, walking time and elevation gain, and with a downloaded map they work without signal too.

03 · Stock

Your pantry in your pocket.

What you have at home, where you keep it, when it expires. Items close to expiry are colour-coded.

04 · Knowledge base

Knowledge that is there even without signal.

21 topics from water to first aid, with the in-depth, detailed articles, readable without internet.

05 · Compass

Phone in your pocket, sound shows the way.

Record your bearing and audio guidance leads you. It shows the way back too. With the camera it shows today's sun path too, for a solar panel.

06 · Bible

Strength for hard days.

The King James Version on your phone, with read-aloud, without internet.

07 · Emergency card

The paramedic gets everything from one QR code.

Blood type, allergies, medication and an emergency contact for every family member, printable too.

08 · SOS

One button for when things go wrong.

Light and sound signals, emergency call and your emergency card on one screen, even without internet.

09 · Morse

A message when nothing else works.

It sends with light and sound, reads with the camera and microphone, and you can practise with it too.

10 · Document wallet

Your documents, behind a PIN.

Photos of your ID, driving licence, health insurance card and insurance, encrypted and only on your phone.

Our promise

It will not stop working when you need it most.

This is an emergency app. There is no moment when you open it in trouble and it asks you to pay first. The torch, SOS, Morse, the emergency card and the basic knowledge base articles work without internet, for everyone, for free.

What can it do?

Six situations, eighteen features.

When the internet goes down

  • Readiness calculationEnter who lives with you (pets too) and what is in the pantry. The app shows, by location and as a percentage, how you stand on water, food, heating and power. If something is running low, you see what to restock.
  • Knowledge base without internet21 topics from water to first aid, including the in-depth, detailed articles. Whatever is on your phone can be read without signal.
  • Local messagesText messages to people near you without internet, over a shared network, even a phone's hotspot.
  • Noah, artificial intelligence without internetIt runs on the phone itself, not in the cloud, so it answers even with no signal. Ask it about the knowledge base, the Bible and your own stock, for example what has expired. It works in the Android app; the language model is downloaded once.

Power cut

  • Torch and red night lightRed light does not dazzle eyes that have adjusted to the dark.
  • Power and heating calculatorHow long a power bank or power station will last, and how much fuel you need for the winter.
  • Stock listWhat you have at home, where you keep it, and when it expires. Items close to expiry are colour-coded.

Water and food

  • Water purification calculatorHow much bleach or how many tablets you need to disinfect a given amount of water.
  • Rainwater calculatorHow much water you can collect from your roof in a given rainfall.
  • “What to pack?” packing listBuilt from your family members, with a weight limit so the pack stays carryable.

No signal

  • Morse and faxMorse code with sound and flashing light, and sending pictures over sound.
  • Secret QRA message, location or list in a QR code that only someone who knows your family password can read.

In the field

  • Map and route planningHiking trails, springs, peaks. Enter where you are going from and to, and the app shows the distance, walking time, elevation gain and the trail markings to follow. You can download the map, and it works in the middle of the woods without signal.
  • Compass with audio guidanceRecord your bearing and the app guides you by sound, so your phone can go in your pocket. It shows the way back too, plus sunrise and the moon phase. In camera view it draws today's sun path with hour marks over the image, so you can aim a solar panel, offline too.
  • Fall detectionIf you stay motionless after a fall and impact, a countdown starts, and unless you cancel it, an SOS message with your location goes out and the emergency whistle sounds.
  • Walking time and lightningHow long it takes to reach your destination on foot, and how far away lightning struck.

Family and faith

  • Family and emergency cardThis is where you add your family: readiness and the packing list are calculated from it. The emergency card holds blood type, allergies, medication and an emergency contact.
  • Bible without internetThe King James Version on your phone, with read-aloud.
  • Emergency whistle and CPR rhythmA loud emergency whistle, and a voice that counts out the CPR rhythm, so you do not have to count in a panic.

Frequently asked questions

Which phones does it work on?

Android and iPhone. Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

Do I need internet?

Not for the most important parts: the torch, SOS, Morse, the emergency card and the basic knowledge base articles work without internet. The free map needs internet; the downloadable map that works offline is part of premium. You also need internet for downloads, updates and redeeming a code.

Where is my data?

On your phone. You can find the details on the Privacy page.

Will anything stop working if I do not buy a code?

No. The free part stays free, and no feature stops working.

Where can I find the user guide?

On the User guide page, and from the Help inside the app.
