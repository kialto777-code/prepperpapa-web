Water purification calculator

If the water supply fails or the source is suspect, there are two proven ways: boiling or chlorine disinfection. With both, the ratio and the time are what count, and these are exactly what people get wrong from memory. Enter how much water you want to treat, and it tells you the exact amount.

The calculation is based on the ratios widely used in Hungarian practice. Filter cloudy water first, because the chlorine gets used up on suspended particles. If the water may have been in contact with sewage, boiling is the safer option.

What it is good for, and what it is not

This calculator comes from the PrepperPapa app, where it is free too. The calculation runs in your browser: we send nothing anywhere and store no data about it.
