Partner program

Recommend PrepperPapa.

If your audience cares about preparedness, recommend the PrepperPapa app to them. You earn a commission on every premium purchase you bring in.

Apply as a partner

You apply on the GoAffPro partner portal.

The terms

  • 20% commission on the premium purchases you bring.
  • With your coupon code the buyer gets 5% off.
  • 30-day cookie: a purchase counts as yours for 30 days after someone clicks your link.
  • For now for companies and sole traders only.
  • We approve every application individually, by hand.
  • Payout is made by bank transfer, against an invoice, once your accumulated commission reaches €100.

How it works, step by step

  1. Application: you register on the partner portal with the button above.
  2. Approval: we review your application and let you know by e-mail once it is approved.
  3. Your own link and coupon: you get your personal link and coupon code on the partner portal.
  4. Sale: when someone arrives through your link or buys with your coupon, their premium purchase is credited to you.
  5. 30-day hold: the commission stays pending for 30 days after the purchase, so that any refund can be settled.
  6. Invoice: you issue an invoice for the approved commission. We send you the billing details when you are approved.
  7. Payout: we transfer the commission based on your invoice.

Any questions?

If your application does not go through, or you have a question, write to prepperpapa@gmail.com with the subject “Partnerprogram”.
